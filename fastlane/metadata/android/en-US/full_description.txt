Trips, flatmates, nights out: someone always pays, and someone has to work out who owes what. Balnana does the maths.

Share a link with your group and add expenses as they happen. Balnana shows everyone where they stand, and the few payments that settle it all.

SPLIT IT YOUR WAY
• Equally, by exact amounts or by shares
• Several payers on one expense
• Refunds and income, not just spending
• Recurring expenses such as rent or subscriptions
• Pin where it happened on a map and attach a photo of the receipt

SETTLE UP IN A FEW PAYMENTS
• Balnana works out a short list of payments that squares everyone up
• Record a payment once the money has changed hands, and balances update for everyone

BUILT FOR TRAVEL
• Enter an expense in the currency you paid in, and balances stay in the group's currency
• Type the exchange rate, or what it came to on your card statement
• Saved groups still open when you're offline

EASY TO SHARE
• Invite friends with a private link or a QR code
• See expenses appear as others add them, with push notifications

KNOW WHERE THE MONEY WENT
• Spending by category, by member and by month
• Export to CSV, or bring your groups over from Settle Up

OPEN SOURCE, NO ADS
Balnana has no ads and no tracking. Use the official server at app.balnana.com, or connect to any other Balnana server. The app and the server are open source under the AGPL-3.0: https://github.com/willie6666/balnana

Push notifications use UnifiedPush. If you don't get them, install a distributor such as ntfy.
