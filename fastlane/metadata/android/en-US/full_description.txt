Balnana is a simple and easy-to-use shared expense tracker that helps you manage expenses with friends, family, and travel companions.

Whether you're traveling together, sharing meals, living with roommates, or organizing group activities, Balnana makes it easier to keep track of shared expenses without the hassle of managing spreadsheets or scattered notes.

✨ Features

📒 Group Expense Tracking
Create separate groups for trips, everyday life, or special activities. Keep all your shared expenses organized in one place.

💰 Easy Expense Splitting
Record shared expenses and splitting details. Keep track of everyone's expenses and shares without complicated manual calculations.

🧾 Receipt & Image Attachments
Attach receipts and images to expenses for easy reference. No more searching through chat histories for missing receipts.

👥 Collaborative Groups
Invite other members to your groups and work together to record and view shared expenses, keeping everyone up to date.
